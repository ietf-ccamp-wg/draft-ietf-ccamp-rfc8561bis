Title:
IETF CCAMP WG newly adopted working group draft updating Microwave Radio Link YANG Model

Body:

The editors of A [YANG Data Model for Microwave Radio Link](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-ccamp-rfc8561bis/) would like to inform ETSI ATTM TM4 about the use of ETSI EN 302 217-2.

During the revision of the YANG data model, the editors reviewed the use of the reference-modulation-index against Table 0 of ETSI EN 302 217-2.

The editors' understanding is that the reference-modulation-index represents the minimum spectrum efficiency class to be fulfilled by the radio link configuration, together with the characteristics associated with that class, such as system capacity, spectral efficiency class, and spectrum mask for the applicable channel separation. These characteristics are those used in the link-by-link coordination and licensing process.

Where a spectrum efficiency class is divided into subclasses A and B, the editors understand the requirements to be cumulative, such that the radio link configuration is expected to fulfill both subclasses.

The proposed change to the draft reflects this understanding and is intended to remove ambiguity in the description of reference-modulation-index.

We look forward to any comments or clarifications to our understanding related to our interpretation used in our modeling.
